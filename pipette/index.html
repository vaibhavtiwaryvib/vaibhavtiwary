Features

Everything a typical experiment needs, nothing more

I built Pipette for my own fly-lab data: a familiar spreadsheet-and-graph workflow, the statistical tests biologists actually use, and graphs that go straight into a figure or a slide.

Data tables

  • Grouped: one column per group, replicates in rows
  • Two-way: a row factor (e.g. genotype) × groups (e.g. treatment)
  • Survival: time + 1 = death / 0 = censored
  • Paste straight from Excel, including headers and comma decimals
  • Select like a spreadsheet — drag, Shift-click, whole rows or columns — then copy, paste, clear or delete with one key
  • Exclude values the Prism way (shown in blue, left out of the analysis)
  • Open GraphPad Prism files (.pzfx; .prism experimental) — column, grouped and survival data tables come straight in

Statistics

  • Unpaired, Welch’s and paired t tests; Mann–Whitney; Wilcoxon
  • One-way, Welch’s and repeated-measures ANOVA; Kruskal–Wallis; Friedman
  • Multiple comparisons like Prism: every pair, each vs. a control, or only the pairs you pick — Tukey, Dunnett, Šídák, Holm–Šídák, Bonferroni, Fisher’s LSD, Games–Howell, Dunn
  • Two-way ANOVA, Shapiro–Wilk normality test, descriptive statistics

Graphs

  • Scatter dot plots, bars, box & whiskers, violin, before–after, mean ± error
  • Significance brackets with stars or P values, added automatically
  • Clustered bars, dots and lines for two-way data
  • Classic, bold or thin styles; colorblind-safe palettes

Survival & export

  • Kaplan–Meier curves with censor ticks, n and median survival on the graph
  • Log-rank and Gehan–Breslow–Wilcoxon tests, hazard ratio, pairwise comparisons
  • Number-at-risk table
  • Export PNG, TIFF, JPEG (150–600 dpi) or vector PDF and SVG

Install

Installing takes a minute

Pipette is free and not sold through the App Store or Microsoft Store, so the first launch needs one extra click to confirm you trust it.

Mac

  1. Open Pipette.dmg and drag Pipette into Applications.
  2. Double-click Pipette. macOS will say it can’t verify the developer.
  3. macOS 15 or newer: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
    macOS 14 or older: right-click Pipette → Open → Open.

A real Mac app: its own window, menus and Save/Open dialogs — no browser needed. Works fully offline, macOS 11 or newer.

Windows

  1. Download Pipette.exe and double-click it.
  2. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” screen appears, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Pipette opens in its own window, with File / Edit / View menus and normal Save and Open dialogs.

A real Windows app — no browser needed. No installer, no admin rights, works offline. Windows 10 or 11 (it uses the Microsoft Edge web engine that comes with Windows). Keep Pipette.exe anywhere, e.g. your Desktop or Documents.

Good to know

Private, checked, and free

Your data stays with you

Everything runs on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded. Save projects as .pipette files and reopen them on Mac or Windows.

Statistics are verified

Every test was checked against established statistics packages (scipy, statsmodels, pingouin) on many random datasets, matching to many decimal places.

Free and open

Pipette is free for everyone and released under the MIT licence. Feedback and suggestions are welcome by email.

See exactly how it works

Pipette’s full source code is public, so you don’t have to take the statistics on trust. The whole app is one readable file: the statistical tests, the graphs and the export are all in plain JavaScript. The Mac and Windows downloads run this same code inside a small native window (Mac source, Windows source).
